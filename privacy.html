Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Prime Buck Junk Removal ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through primebuckjunkremoval.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, phone number, email address (optional), a description of the items you need removed, and any photos you choose to attach. If you opt in, we also record your consent to receive text messages.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and schedule service
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your request
  • To keep basic records of past and prospective customers

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Text messages (SMS)

If you opt in to receive text messages, we use that number solely to communicate about your quote or service. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for assistance. Consent to receive texts is never required to purchase our services.

Data retention

We retain quote requests and related communications for as long as reasonably necessary for business, scheduling, and record-keeping purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information from our records at any time by contacting us using the details below.
