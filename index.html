Questions we get asked

Which areas do you serve?

Jacksonville and the surrounding communities, including Jacksonville Beach, Atlantic Beach, Ponte Vedra Beach, Ponte Vedra, St. Augustine, St. Augustine Beach, Orange Park, Fleming Island, St. Johns County and Middleburg. If you are nearby, ask and we will tell you straight away.

What furniture will you take?

Furniture removal is our most requested service. Couches, beds, dressers, tables, chairs and other household pieces, whether it is one item or an entire room. Tell us what you have and we will confirm.

How is pricing worked out?

Price depends on the volume and size of what is going, meaning how much truck space it takes up. Access matters too. If items are hard to reach or need extra effort to get out, that affects the cost. You get a clear estimate for your specific job before we start.

Do you remove appliances?

Yes, from refrigerators to washers and dryers. We are flexible on timing, so if you need something picked up soon, get in touch and we will work around your availability.

What does an estate cleanout include?

We remove furniture, appliances, personal items and general debris from the whole property, working through it room by room. Our crew handles the heavy lifting and the hauling. It is the full solution when a home needs emptying.

Can you come on short notice?

We keep scheduling flexible. Whether it is a last minute cleanup or something planned well ahead, we will do our best to fit you in. Get in touch and we will find a time.
